# Quickstart: Budget and Itinerary Generation

Prerequisites: plans 001–005 running; a trip with destination, dates, adults, budget, **at least one chosen place to visit** (plan 005, Places to visit tab; Updated 2026-10-13) and, optionally, a selected hotel and transport option. Knowledge for the destination need not be ingested: places come live from OpenStreetMap / Wikidata.

## Validate
0. **Gate and auto-generate** (added 2026-10-13): with no chosen place, `/trip/<id>` shows "Choose places to visit first" and a button to the Places tab. After choosing places, opening the plan builds the itinerary by itself (no Generate button); change the chosen places and reopen: it is rebuilt; leave them unchanged and reopen: edits (deleted items) are kept.
1. **Planner layout**: open `/trip/<id>`; bands in order hero picture, header (destination, dates, **Download**), (banner), two columns with a 320px budget column (budget, Selected hotels, Selected transport, Places to visit); compare with `docs/UI-SPEC.md` §2.5.
2. **Budget sums**: in psql run `SELECT * FROM trip_budget_summary('<id>')` and `SELECT * FROM trip_budget_totals('<id>')`; category amounts add up to `estimated_total`, and `remaining = budget - estimated_total`; the UI shows the same strings.
3. **No real figure**: Transportation shows the fare estimate with the Estimated badge (source "Faro estimate, not a quote"). Select a transport option whose distance cannot be determined (or delete the FX rate it needs): the category shows its sum with "(partial)" (or an em dash when it has no items), is listed in `categories_without_real_figure`, and the total says partial; the amount is not counted as zero. The Estimated total, Budget and Remaining are shown for every state.
4. **Currency**: set the budget currency different from a hotel's currency; the converted amount shows Estimated and `fx_rate_id` points at a stored Frankfurter rate; with no stored rate it is Unavailable.
5. **Over budget**: lower the budget below the total; the banner shows the overage and proposal rows with savings. Check one row and press **Apply selected**; only that proposal applies, totals update, the banner hides once within budget. With no provable saving the banner says so.
6. **Itinerary rules**: every activity with stated hours lies inside them for that weekday (`opening_hours_checked = true`), travel time to the next stop is shown, and meals/rests appear each day. Manually set an attraction's hours to closed on one weekday; regenerating that day does not schedule it.
7. **Edits**: delete an activity; the day re-times and the budget changes; swap to a closed attraction; refused with a message.
8. **Unknown hours** (changed 2026-10-13): ~~an attraction with unparseable `opening_hours` is never scheduled~~. A chosen place that states no (or unreadable) hours is scheduled, stored with `opening_hours_checked = false`, shown as "Opening hours not stated: check before you go", and the generation note says so; no hours are invented. With no chosen places, only places with readable hours are planned. Swapping to an attraction without readable hours is refused (422).
9. **Failure**: block the Overpass host; generation reports that attractions are unavailable; no invented activities; last good plan remains.
10. **Selected lists**: hotel dot is hollow until a booking is confirmed (after plan 007), then filled; transport dot is always hollow (a booked Duffel test flight reads "booked, reference ..."; trains and buses read "compared only, not booked"); the Places to visit list shows the chosen places or "None chosen yet.".
11. **Signed out**: every step redirects to `/login` (login first).
